# CareRoute demo – voice-over script (≈3 minutes)

Read at a calm pace (about 140 words a minute). **Bold** = stress the word. `[ … ]` = what is on screen; don't read it.
Each block fits the time on the left. If you fall behind, skip the lines marked *(optional)*.

---

**0:00 – 0:06** `[Welcome screen]`

> Millions of Canadians don't have a family doctor.
> When they get sick, the hardest question is: **where do I go?**

**0:06 – 0:33** `[My household – add Lina, then Little Chrisny]`

> CareRoute answers that in under a minute.
> First, I add my household – once.
> Lina: her birth date, no family doctor, and her health card.
> And her baby, Little Chrisny, twelve months old.
> All of this stays **on this phone**. Nothing is saved on our servers.

**0:33 – 0:45** `[Who needs care? → Lina]`

> Now I just tap **who** needs care.
> CareRoute already knows her age and that she has no family doctor –
> so it **never asks again**.

**0:45 – 1:03** `[Safety check → Arabic message → emergency screen in Arabic]`

> Safety comes first. Before any AI runs, fixed rules check for emergencies.
> Watch: I type chest pain in **Arabic**…
> CareRoute stops right away and tells her, **in Arabic**, to call 9-1-1.
> No AI guessing – just clear, life-saving instructions.

**1:03 – 1:24** `[Go back → "burning and urgent urination" → Moderate · About the same · 1–3 days → Find care]`

> Now a common problem: burning when she pees.
> She types it in her own words,
> then three quick taps: how bad, is it changing, and since when.

**1:24 – 1:33** `[One quick question: Could you be pregnant?]`

> CareRoute asks **only** questions that can change where she should go –
> never more than two.

**1:33 – 1:48** `[Result: See a pharmacist → Why → other options → nearby pharmacies]`

> The answer: **see a pharmacist**. In Alberta, pharmacists can assess this and prescribe.
> It shows **why**, with a link to the official government source.
> And if things change – a walk-in clinic, or 8-1-1 to talk to a nurse.
> Nearby pharmacies, open now, with distance, phone and directions.

**1:48 – 2:24** `[Book with my calendar → times → Approve & book → Booking confirmed → Getting there]`

> Booking takes **one tap**.
> It checks her calendar and the pharmacy's hours, and suggests times that work for both.
> She picks Friday, approves, and it's booked – with her details already filled in.
> It's added to her calendar,
> and she gets a bus route timed to arrive early – or an Uber, one tap away.

**2:24 – 2:54** `[Real pharmacy → Call helper → reason for visit → "What to say"]`

> Not every pharmacy books online. So for a phone call, CareRoute writes the script:
> the store, the reason for the visit, and **exactly what to say**.
> That matters for newcomers, or anyone nervous about calling.
> *(optional)* Every line has a copy button.

**2:54 – end** `[Health summaries]`

> And she can carry a one-page health summary to any provider – so she never starts from zero.
>
> CareRoute: **the right care, the first time** – in her language, in under a minute.

---

## If you're asked a question

- **"Does it diagnose?"** No. It never says what you have. It only points to the right service, and shows the official source for every answer.
- **"Is the AI making the safety call?"** No. Emergencies are caught by fixed rules before any AI runs, and checked again after.
- **"Where is my data?"** On the phone, only if you agree. Nothing about your health is stored on our servers.
- **"Which provinces?"** Alberta and British Columbia today; the rules are written so more provinces can be added.
