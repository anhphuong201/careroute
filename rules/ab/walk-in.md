# AB – Walk-in clinic or urgent care centre

## When this route fits
- Walk-in clinic: non-emergency care without an appointment for minor illnesses, injuries and general health, including renewals and lab follow-up when there is no family doctor.
- Urgent care centre: problems that are not life-threatening but need care the same day, evenings or weekends. Urgent care wait times are published by AHS.

## When this route does NOT fit
- Signs of an emergency → call 911.
- Not sure how urgent it is → call Health Link 811 first.

## What to do
- Check the clinic's hours before going. Try to use the same walk-in clinic each time so it keeps your records.
- Bring: health card, medicine list, CareRoute one-page summary.
