# AB – Contact your own family doctor or nurse practitioner first

## What Primary Care Alberta says
- Family doctors provide ongoing, complete care for people of all ages and support specialist or hospital care when needed. If your doctor needs advice from a specialist, they can refer you.

## When this route fits
- The person HAS a family doctor or nurse practitioner, and can reach them in the time they need.
- Lab result follow-up: results go to the provider who ordered the test, so that provider is the best first contact.

## When this route does NOT fit
- No family doctor, or the doctor cannot see them in time → use pharmacist, virtual care or walk-in instead.

## What to do
- Call the clinic and ask for an appointment or a phone visit.
- Say: "I need [a prescription renewal / to talk about my test results]. When is the soonest I can be seen?"
