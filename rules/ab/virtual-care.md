# AB – Virtual care (phone or video)

## What Primary Care Alberta says
- Virtual care connects patients with doctors online or by phone for non-emergency medical care, such as consultations, prescriptions or follow-ups.
- **Virtual MD** (through Health Link 811) lets patients consult a doctor by video or phone, get prescriptions and other services without visiting a clinic or hospital.

## When this route fits
- The person needs a doctor but not in person: renewals a pharmacist cannot do, talking about lab results, sick notes, lab requisitions, referrals.
- Rural areas or no transport.
- Health Link 811 runs Virtual MD: after a call, some 811 callers can talk to a doctor by phone or video.
- Several virtual clinics (for example Telus Health MyCare, Maple, Tiahealth, Rocket Doctor) are listed by Navigate Care Alberta as covered by the Alberta Health Care Insurance Plan. Check coverage before booking.

## When this route does NOT fit
- Anything that needs a physical exam, test or procedure today → walk-in or urgent care.

## What to do
- Have the Alberta Health Care card, medicine list and any recent test results ready.
