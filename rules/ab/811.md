# AB – Health Link 811 (free, 24/7 nurse advice)

## What Primary Care Alberta says
- Health Link 811 is a free 24/7 phone service connecting patients with health professionals for advice and help finding care: nurse advice for you or a loved one, addiction and mental health support, and new parent and newborn support (children younger than two).
- If you are unsure whether you should go to urgent care, call Health Link for advice.

## When this route fits
- The person is not sure how urgent the problem is, or which service to use.
- Questions about a baby or child's health. (New Parent and Newborn Line for babies under 2 months: 1-833-805-2229.)
- Health Link registered nurses give advice on symptoms, help decide whether urgent care is needed, and connect to local services. Interpretation is available.
- Mental health and addiction helplines are also reached through 811, 24/7.

## When this route does NOT fit
- Signs of an emergency → call 911.

## What to do
- Dial 8-1-1. From a VoIP / web phone, call 1-866-408-5465.
- Say: "I need an interpreter for [language]" if needed.
