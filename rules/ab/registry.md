# AB – Find a family doctor (Alberta Find a Provider)

## When this route fits
- The person has no family doctor or nurse practitioner. Always offer this IN ADDITION to the route for today's need.

## What to do
- Search albertafindaprovider.ca for doctors and nurse practitioners accepting new patients nearby, or ask them to contact you.
- If no one is available, call Health Link 811 and ask to be added to the Alberta Find a Doctor list. Have the Alberta Health Care card number ready.
- Being on the list does not stop you from using pharmacists, virtual care or walk-in clinics.
