# BC – 8-1-1 virtual physician (phone or video)

## When this route fits
- After talking with an 8-1-1 nurse, the person may be referred to a HealthLink BC doctor by phone or video, 7 days a week, 9 am to 11 pm PT.
- Good for advice on when and where to seek care, and for questions after a test, when there is no family doctor.

## When this route does NOT fit
- Regular prescription refills or sick notes: HealthLink BC doctors do not provide these → pharmacist or walk-in.
- Emergencies → 9-1-1.

## What to do
- Call 8-1-1 and explain the concern to the nurse. The physician calls back (the number may show a US area code).
