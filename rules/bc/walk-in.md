# BC – Walk-in clinic or Urgent and Primary Care Centre (UPCC)

## When this route fits
- Walk-in clinic: non-urgent care, advice, assessment and treatment for minor illness and injuries, often evenings and weekends, usually no appointment needed. Also renewals a pharmacist cannot do, and lab follow-up without a family doctor.
- UPCC: same-day, non-life-threatening problems that need care within 12–24 hours, such as minor injuries, sprains, infections, fevers, cuts, less serious child illness. Open evenings, weekends and holidays (hours vary).

## When this route does NOT fit
- Major accident or possibly life-threatening symptoms → 9-1-1 / emergency room.
- Not sure if it is an emergency → call 8-1-1.

## What to do
- Check hours first. Bring: BC Services Card / PHN, medicine list, CareRoute one-page summary.
