# BC – HealthLink BC 8-1-1 (free, 24/7)

## When this route fits
- The person is not sure how urgent it is or where to go.
- A health service navigator answers 24/7 and can transfer to a registered nurse (24/7), pharmacist or dietitian, and find nearby walk-in clinics.
- Translation in over 130 languages: say the language (for example, "Punjabi") and an interpreter joins.

## When this route does NOT fit
- Signs of an emergency → 9-1-1.

## What to do
- Dial 8-1-1 (7-1-1 for deaf and hard of hearing; 604-215-8110 from web phones).
