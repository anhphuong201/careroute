# BC – Contact your own family doctor or nurse practitioner first

## When this route fits
- The person HAS a family doctor or nurse practitioner and can reach them in the time they need.
- Primary care includes renewing a prescription, routine check-ups and following up on tests.

## When this route does NOT fit
- No family doctor, or not reachable in time → pharmacist, walk-in / UPCC or 811.

## What to do
- Call the clinic: "I need [a prescription renewal / to talk about my test results]. When is the soonest I can be seen?"
